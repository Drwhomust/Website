# What is my art sytle in the first place?

A lot of people say my art style is unique in some way.

For me I think it's the same.

Just your standard emo style that a kid in a black hoodie wearing chains and knee high converse would draw in math class
when they kid board of the lesson. Nothing speical. The only true speical thing about my version of it is that i combind.
Elements from the video game pizza tower. Just so I can have a good mixture of emo and goofyness. Plus, I really like pizza
tower!

But yet we are here. So here is my guide to it (this is mostly tips and trick to get somewhat close to mine. I **HIGHY RECOMMEND** watching the video above
and expermenting how you should draw it! I am just providing a guide to make drawing it somewhat easier or if you want to make stuff that's like the same stuff
i made. This is not a how to, just me explaining how i do things since people get curious)

With that out of the way

**HERE WE GO!!!**

# eyes and face

For the eyes and face what I would do is to fill in the circles and add some extra rings around them. Also leave some gaps in between
Some of the lines to make it look like your drawing on paper and did it in a rush while the teacher was looking

![](/media/drawing_emo_eyes.GIF)

![](/media/emo_eyes.png)

For the mouth I would try to do a cute look of a emoticon like :D or :3 and :O etc

some examples:

![](/media/emo_faces.png)

## Hair and stuff

# Body

It's hard to say what to do with the body since I don't really do a good job with posing or making the body do stuff

## Clothing

You can do whatever you want with clothing but a tip is to make it look cartoony and chaotic. It brings out personailty

### bonus tip

try to draw in [scene](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=scenecore%20clothing&iar=images&t=ffab) or [emo](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=emo%20clothing&iar=images&t=ffab) clothing since this is what the art style was made for. But again, your the one holding the pencial. so do
whatever you want. Draw them in the most colorful scene clothing or have them wear a duck costume. Up to you!

## Face

as for the face,

eyes try to keep them as far from each other to make them look dumb and have no thoughts behind that silly head

ears, make them tall and pointy and add a bit of fuff

hair: it's optional but if you really want to add some then do something like [this](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=guy%20hairstyles&iar=images&t=ffab&iai=https%3A%2F%2Fallysonstyles.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2023%2F12%2Fdamian-barczak-5st86wYikQQ-unsplash-scaled.jpg)

and for mouth make them do that :3 or really any funny face you want.
